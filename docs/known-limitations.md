# Known Limitations

- This scenario demonstrates responsibility boundaries, not performance or retrieval-quality superiority.
- The application resolver independently validates the lifecycle metadata and preserves the current final top result in the reviewed layered-ranking contract.
- “Most similar” means the highest BGE cross-encoder rerank score in this scenario. It does not mean the Run A dense-cosine leader or the final Full Stack top result.
- The pinned Cortrix source may expose known observability gaps. A run must disclose them rather than silently treating them as successful product claims.
- Cost reporting may be partial when internal provider usage is not observable. Partial visibility must not become a public cost claim.
- Licensing and redistribution rights are pending provenance review.
